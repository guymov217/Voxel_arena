# WRECKSMITH: the Steam page kit

Copy-ready text for the store page (Steamworks → *Store presence → Edit store page*), the
tags, the art sizes and the trailer plan. The screenshots and capsules are made from the game
itself: `docs/store/screenshots/` and `docs/store/capsules/` (how to remake them at the end).

---

## Name

**WRECKSMITH**

## Release

**Version 1.0**, a full release (not Early Access), for **Windows and Linux** (64-bit; the Linux build runs in Steam's Linux
Runtime "sniper"). No macOS build for now. No Next Fest for now. Single-player, online co-op and PvP, all in the one build
(the page may only name what the uploaded build has: Valve's build review checks it).

## Genre, in one line (for the page, press and Next Fest)

A **physics-based melee action game** set in a Roman arena: a gladiator brawler where you
**build your own weapons voxel by voxel** and wield them with direct mouse-driven arm control,
against men, beasts and mythical monsters, in a single-player campaign, an online co-op campaign
for up to 4 players, or online PvP and party modes for up to 8.

## Short description (300 characters at most; this is 244)

> Forge a weapon voxel by voxel, then swing it with your own two hands. A physics gladiator game
> where every blow is real: blades bite, bones crack, limbs come off. Fight men, beasts and
> monsters, alone or with friends, and earn the crowd's roar.

## About this game (Steamworks BBCode)

```
[h2]Build it. Swing it. Wreck everything.[/h2]
WRECKSMITH is a physics gladiator game. There are no canned attacks: [b]your mouse is your arm[/b]. Swing hard and the blade bites deep; swing lazily and it glances off. Every fighter, beast and monster is made of voxels, and [b]every voxel can be cut, crushed or torn away[/b].

[h2]What you do[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Forge[/b] a weapon at the smith, voxel by voxel: pick the material of every block, which part cuts, stabs or crushes, and where your hand holds it.
[*][b]Fight[/b] with it in the arena. Move with the keys, steer each hand with the mouse, turn the edge with the wheel, thrust, kick, grab and throw.
[*][b]Survive[/b] what you have done: wounds bleed, broken limbs stay broken, weapons chip and dull. The physician patches you up between bouts, for a price.
[*][b]Spend[/b] your purse on armour you design yourself, pots of Greek fire and nails, and better steel, then climb the next stage of the campaign, alone or with friends.
[/list]

[h2]The forge[/h2]
[list]
[*]Build any weapon voxel by voxel: swords, axes, hammers, a two-person sword, a goose.
[*]Each material has its own weight, edge and strength. The centre of mass and the grip are yours to set.
[*]Hone the edge as keen as your purse allows: the sharper the blade, the more every edge block costs. Sharp blocks are drawn as real wedges and points.
[*]Set gems in the blade: ruby burns, sapphire freezes, emerald poisons.
[*]Weapons dull, chip and finally break where you used them most. Mend them, or forge a better one.
[/list]

[h2]Every blow is physical[/h2]
[list]
[*]Cuts, thrusts and blunt blows each wound in their own way, voxel by voxel.
[*]Limbs hang, swing and come off. Pick one up and hit someone with it.
[*]Organs, bones and blood under the skin: a crushed rib, a punctured lung, an artery that will not stop.
[*]Wrestle: grab, shove, trip. Run out of breath and every swing gets weaker.
[/list]

[h2]Men, beasts and monsters[/h2]
Lions, bears, crocodiles that drag you into a death roll, elephants that charge and stomp, a gorilla that tears men in two, a scorpion whose sting poisons. Then the myths: the Cyclops, the Minotaur, Cerberus, the Hydra, the Titan and a dragon. 25 creatures, each with attacks you can see coming and must answer with steel. Every part of every beast breaks on its own: club a bull's horn off, cripple a lion's leg, cut the stinger from the scorpion.

[h2]The campaign[/h2]
Climb 64 stages, from a drunk in the sand to the Hydra, with 9 bosses on the way. Between bouts you walk the Ludus yard: the board by the arena gate names your next opponent, and you spend your purse at the smith, the armourer and the physician. Wounds stay with you, and so do the scars.

[h2]With friends[/h2]
[list]
[*]Up to 8 players over Steam: a free-for-all, a brawl where everyone fights everyone, or any other arena mode. The co-op campaign takes up to 4.
[*]14 arena and party modes: duels, best of three, teams, king of the hill, head football, last limb standing and more.
[*]The Emperor's decrees change the rules mid-fight. The crowd throws things.
[*]A kill cam replays the best kill of every fight in slow motion. Clip it and share it.
[/list]
```

## Content survey (mature content)

The game shows strong realistic-style violence on voxel figures: severed limbs, organs and blood.
Answer **"Frequent violence or gore"** truthfully. It puts the page behind an age gate in some
countries but not off the store. Mention the in-game Gore setting (Full or Reduced).

## System requirements (check on a low-end PC before publishing)

| | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 10/11 64-bit |
| Processor | 4 cores, 3.0 GHz (Intel i5-6600 / Ryzen 3 1300X) | 6 cores (Intel i5-10400 / Ryzen 5 3600) |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM |
| Graphics | Vulkan 1.1 or Direct3D 12 GPU, 2 GB (GTX 960 / RX 470) | GTX 1660 / RX 5600 XT, 6 GB |
| Storage | 1 GB | 1 GB |
| Network | Broadband for online play | Broadband for online play |
| Notes | Graphics quality: Low | Graphics quality: High |

**Linux** (second tab of the same form): **SteamOS or a 64-bit Linux that runs Steam** (Ubuntu 20.04 or later), the same
processor, memory and graphics as above, with a **Vulkan 1.1** driver (Mesa 21 or later, or NVIDIA 470 or later). Valve's own Linux
runtime ("sniper", glibc 2.31) carries the rest.

The physics is the heavy part, on the processor: 8 fighters at once run at about 7.4 ms per
physics step on one core. (These figures are an estimate from one machine: `RELEASE_HE.md`, stage 2, asks for measured ones on
a weak, a middle and a strong PC before the form is filled in.)

before the page names them.

## AI disclosure (Steam's content survey)

Valve asks whether AI was used to make content the player sees or hears. For this game the honest answers are:

- **Interface translations (the 15 non-English languages): written with an AI and not yet read by native speakers.** Tick
  *pre-generated content* and name localization, or have the languages checked by people first and then describe what was
  checked. This is the one that matters for this page.
- Voices and sounds: recordings in the public domain or CC0, processed by the game's own tools (`art/CREDITS.md`).
- Music: sampled real instruments played by the game's tools (`art/CREDITS.md`).
- Pictures, models and textures: generated by the scripts in `tools/` (`gen_voxel_art.py`, `gen_textures.py`, `make_app_icon.py`...; `art/CREDITS.md`), and the store art is the game's own frames. Say so only if nothing else was made with an image generator: you know that, I do not.
- A coding assistant was used while writing the game: Valve's note of January 2026 says tools that only help make the game, and
  whose output the player never sees, are not what the question is about.

Answer for yourself at the form: this section is what I know, not legal advice.

## The trailer (75 seconds; the first 5 decide)

Steam autoplays the first trailer muted, so the first seconds must read without sound: it opens
on a blow landing, and its lines are on screen.

`tools\make_trailer75.bat` makes it from the game itself on a PC with a graphics card, shot by
shot to its production script: `docs\store\trailer75.mp4` (1920x1080, H.264, AAC, ready to
upload), a version without text beside it, and the cut list. The shots, the rules they are made
by and what each shows: `tools/TRAILER.md`.

## Remaking the screenshots and capsules

On a PC with a graphics card, double-click **`tools\make_screenshots.bat`** (a few minutes; `make_screenshots.bat 03_clash` makes one).
The title, your-gladiator and forge pictures here are in the current look; the fights were last made before the characters were
redrawn, so **run the batch once on a PC with a GPU, pick the best eight to ten, and replace the rest** before the page goes up
(a machine with no GPU needs many minutes for each fight).

```
# screenshots (needs a display or xvfb-run), 1920x1080
SHOT_DIR=docs/store/screenshots godot --resolution 1920x1080 --path . -- --probe steamshots
# the logo (logo.png, logo_stacked.png, library_logo_1280x720.png), then the capsules: the
# key-art frames in docs/store/keyart (tests/capsuleart.gd) with the logo on them; the main
# capsule's frame (keyart/giant_main.jpg) is cap_giant_main_2.png from
#   CAPS_SCENE=giant CAPS_FRAMING=side CAPS_VIEWS=main SHOT_DIR=/tmp/k godot --path . -- --probe capsuleart
python3 tools/make_logo.py
python3 tools/make_capsules.py
# the key art: the poster frame and the wide frame, then the posters
SHOT_DIR=/tmp/ka KEYART_VIEW=poster godot --resolution 1200x1800 --path . -- --probe keyart
SHOT_DIR=/tmp/ka KEYART_VIEW=wide godot --resolution 2560x1080 --path . -- --probe keyart
python3 tools/make_keyart.py /tmp/ka docs/store/capsules
```
