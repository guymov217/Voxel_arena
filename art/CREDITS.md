  game.

## Voices (`audio/voices/*.ogg`)

Every beast, monster, giant and man has a voice of its own, built by `tools/build_voices.py` from
CC0 / public-domain recordings of the real animals (pitched, layered and trimmed for the arena; the
beasts of myth are layered from the animals they are made of: a chimera from a lion, a goat and a
snake, a basilisk from a serpent and a cock, a cerberus from three hounds). All outputs are CC0.
The processed cuts come from [hoffspot/conquest](https://github.com/hoffspot/conquest)
(`client/sounds`, CC0), which made them from these recordings:

The men's grunts, cries and deaths also use the Warfork player voices (`sounds/players/male`, Team
Forbidden LLC, CC0) and The Motion Monkey's Free Retro Arcade Sounds Pack (vocal pack, CC0), both by
way of [lavenderdotpet/cc0-public-domain-sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/cc0-public-domain-sounds).
The hyena's giggle and whoop are a hound's yelps, quickened and climbing; the elephant's and the
mammoth's trumpets and rumbles (`tools/elephant_voice.py`), the gorilla's chest beat and the bull's
snort are made in code.
